# Code of Conduct

Resume Fit is for people who are job hunting, which is often stressful. The people who build it should be easy to work with.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Be welcoming to people at every level, including first-time contributors.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind. Accept it the same way.
- Keep private information private. Do not post anyone's real resume, contact details or employment history. Use fictional examples.

## What we will not accept

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on the repository, or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential.

This is a short, plain-language version. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
